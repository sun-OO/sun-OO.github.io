Predictions need validation.

I am a B.Pharm student whose research starts at the bench and in animal models, and uses computation to decide what is worth testing. I am not interested in prediction alone: a candidate means little until it has been tested in a living system.

My interests are phytopharmacology, neuropharmacology and computational drug discovery. I also work as a teaching assistant for computational biology classes.

Islamic University, Kushtia, Bangladesh. Working in research since May 2024. Looking toward Master's and PhD research.

What I do

Experimental pharmacology

Dosing and assessing Swiss albino mice under ethics approval, testing plant extracts and fractions for analgesic, antidiabetic, antidiarrhoeal and CNS/PNS activity, alongside antioxidant assays.

Computational drug discovery

Compound profiling, ADMET filtering and docking to rank candidates, so that bench and animal work goes to the compounds most worth testing.

Teaching assistance

Supporting participants at DeepBio Academy with assignments, class notices and learning materials.
